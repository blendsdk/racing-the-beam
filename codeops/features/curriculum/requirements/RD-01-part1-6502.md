## Feature Overview

Part 1 builds the 6502 programming skills every later part depends on: control flow, the stack
and subroutines, bitwise logic, addressing modes, and table-driven code. Lessons 01–03 (Part 0)
and 04–06 (registers/memory, arithmetic, flags/comparisons) are already complete; this RD covers
the remaining lessons 07–11 so Part 1 is finished.

## Learning Outcomes

- The reader can write conditional branches and loops that terminate correctly for every relevant flag state.
- The reader can use the stack correctly: pushes/pulls and nested `JSR`/`RTS` subroutines.
- The reader can set, clear, and test bit flags with `AND`, `ORA`, `EOR`, and shifts.
- The reader can choose addressing modes deliberately and explain why each is required or useful.
- The reader can build lookup tables and index them from 6502 code.
