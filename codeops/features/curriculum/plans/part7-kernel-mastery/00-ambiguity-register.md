**Why the Part 7 evidence is structured this way (AR #6, #29–#31).** A kernel is temporal: a
scanline's stores, a scroll step, a flicker parity, and a text shift are values at a frame. The
state log proves the register/RAM value and the frame; the static ledger proves the cycle and
line arithmetic; the screenshot proves the picture the reader sees. A screenshot never proves the
timing, and the lesson documents say so. This is the Part 2–6 method carried forward.
