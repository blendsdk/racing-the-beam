definition of done. The plan does not reopen them.

**Why the audio evidence is structured this way (AR #6, #22).** A PNG cannot carry a waveform, and
the repository has no audio-capture tool (AR-7). The honest evidence is therefore three-layered:
the state log proves the exact register values and their frame timing; the static ledger proves the
tone formula and the VBLANK budget; the screenshot proves the monitor state that the reader sees
while the ROM plays. The `<RomPlayer>` is the audible artifact. The lesson documents state this
limitation explicitly rather than implying that a still image proves a sound.
